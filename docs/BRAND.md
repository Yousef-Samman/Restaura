# Restaura brand

## Name
Restaura comes from the Latin *restaurare*, "to restore"; the word "restaurant" has the same root. In code and documents the name is written **Restaura**. In the logo it is set in Roman capitals as **RESTAVRA** (the Latin spelling, U written as V), with **MMXXVI** under a thin brass rule, like a plaque.

## Logo
A flat serving cloche (the dome a restaurant lifts to present a dish) with a small AI spark, in dark olive and brass on an ivory tile.

The app itself keeps its light interface (white cards on a pale page, brass buttons). Orange is reserved for what an agent changed.

## Type
Josefin Sans SemiBold (600), capitals, letter-spacing .14em, set about 4px low next to the mark, for the wordmark. Georgia for the small year line. Manrope for the app: ExtraBold (800) for headings, Regular and SemiBold for body text. Fallback: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.

## Rules
- The cloche is always flat; olive dome, brass details, white highlight.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of the cloche knob.
- Use brass sparingly: one highlight per area, not every button.
